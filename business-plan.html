to grow into teams.

Monetisation roadmap

Twelve months,
four clear stages.

Q1 · Months 1–3

Foundations

  • Measure cost per minute, summary and chat
  • Launch Free and Pro pricing (test ₹399 vs ₹499)
  • Improve onboarding and first-meeting activation
Q2 · Months 4–6

Teams & business

  • Launch Team workspaces and billing
  • Guided trials for startups, agencies and sales teams
  • Optimise conversion; test annual plans and credits
Q3 · Months 7–9

Growth

  • Referral incentives and shareable summaries
  • International messaging and regional pricing
  • Validate CRM / project-management integrations
Q4 · Months 10–12

Scale readiness

  • Security, access controls and retention policies
  • Promote annual and Team plans to business users
  • Full financial review: hire, raise or stay lean
The financial goal

Prove that one paying customer is profitable, then prove that acquiring 100 similar customers is repeatable. Scale only after those two things work.
